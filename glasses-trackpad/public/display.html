shorthand-prompting

Research on trackpad-style shorthand gestures for wearable AI glasses. Instead of voice or a full keyboard, the wearer uses a small capacitive surface (wristband, ring, or phone) to steer a cursor on the glasses' limited display.

Gesture vocabulary under study:

  • one-finger drag — move cursor
  • quick tap — select / activate
  • two-finger drag — scroll content

This mock GitHub page is the target UI for the pointing study: participants navigate tabs, star the repo, and open files using only the trackpad.

Feedback channels: visual (cursor + ripple on the display) and haptic (vibration on the trackpad device, standing in for the glasses' audio cue).

Scroll around… the page is intentionally bigger than the small HUD viewport (both taller and wider) so two-finger panning has something to do in every direction. Lorem research ipsum: latency budgets, Fitts's law on tiny displays, dwell-vs-tap selection, and accidental-touch rejection all go here.
